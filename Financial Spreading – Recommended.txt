Financial Spreading – Recommended Extraction Approach
Hybrid Extraction Model (Accuracy + Efficiency)

1. Structural Parsing (Foundation)

Parse annual report into financial statements, notes, and tables

Preserve page numbers, table boundaries, and units

Create structure-aware chunks (not raw text)

2. RAG for Intelligent Localization

Retrieve only relevant sections:

Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow

Key Notes (Debt, Revenue, PPE, Contingencies, Segments)

Exclude narrative-heavy sections (MD&A, governance)

3. Targeted Structured / Sequential Extraction

Perform sequential extraction only within retrieved tables/notes

Apply predefined spreading schema

Normalize units and reporting periods

4. Validation & Controls

Accounting checks (Assets = Liabilities + Equity)

Cash flow consistency checks

Cross-note reconciliation

Page-level traceability for audit

Outcome

Higher accuracy than pure RAG

Lower cost than full-document sequential extraction

Scalable across issuers, formats, and accounting standards
